All rolling statistics strictly utilized shifted windows.

For Demand Forecasting, we engineered dynamic lag features, 7-day and 14-day rolling statistics, and calendar attributes. We benchmarked a 7-day Moving Average Baseline against Linear Regression and a Random Forest Regressor. Evaluated using MAE, RMSE, and Weighted Absolute Percentage Error—or WAPE—our Random Forest Regressor proved superior, achieving a validation MAE of 0.000006 and WAPE of 0.0083, outperforming Linear Regression whose unconstrained hyperplane produced fractional over-predictions.

For Expiry-Risk Classification, we compared a transparent Rule Baseline against Multinomial Logistic Regression and a Random Forest Classifier across batch quantity, sales velocity, and days to expiry. On the held-out test set, the Random Forest achieved 100% accuracy and a macro F1-score of 1.00, perfectly capturing non-linear threshold boundaries. Meanwhile, Logistic Regression achieved 92% accuracy, struggling with intermediate boundary cases."
